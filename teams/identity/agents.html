How MindSuite studies identity and access

A team of 12 agents studies who can sign in to the system and with which rights, the way an experienced identity and security engineer would: first the facts from the directory and the identity provider, then the role model, then what to clean up and how to move sign-in. It works from read-only exports; it never reads passwords or password hashes, and person names never leave your machine.

Four rules the whole system follows

Metadata onlyAccounts, groups, sign-in dates, MFA registration, policies, certificates. Never passwords or hashes.
Person names stay with youThey appear only in your local report where action is needed; the AI agent sees group names and counts only.
Every claim is citedEach finding points to the export line, or to the findings it is built on.
People decideAgents propose. Your identity team confirms, corrects or rejects each result.

The goal of the identity & access analysis

Know who can sign in, who holds power, what is unprotected, and how sign-in will work in the target. It ends in the Identity & access analysis report with a clean-up list.

Who can sign inAccounts, inactive, shared and service accounts.
Who holds powerAdministrator groups and their members, nested rights.
What is unprotectedMissing MFA, policies off, weak or expired certificates.
How sign-in will workTarget roles, identity provider, service identities, order.

One finding, end to end

  1. The directory reader reads the AD export: svc_settlement is enabled, its password never expires, and it is a member of Domain Admins.
  2. Accounts recognises it as a service account; Privileged access flags a service account with administrator rights.
  3. The migration assessment puts it on the clean-up list and plans a managed identity in the target.
  4. Your identity team confirms the nightly settlement only needs rights on its own database, and the admin rights are removed before the move.

What is running today

All 12 agents run in the same MindSuite agent backend as the other teams. In MindSuite, open Record evidence → Identity & access → Run the identity & access agent team.

11 rule-based agents always run and nothing leaves the backend machine. The role-model agent runs only with a Claude API key on the backend and your consent for that run; it sends group and application names with counts, never person names, passwords or hashes.
